Scope & assumptions: this is a nominal material batching calculator — it does not calculate water quantity, water-cement ratio, moisture correction or design-mix (IS 10262) checks.
  • Cement 1440 kg/m³, sand 1650 kg/m³, bag weight 50 kg, dry-volume factor 1.54 — all fixed, not editable.
  • Coarse-aggregate densities are locked: 10 mm — 1750 kg/m³, 12 mm — 1600 kg/m³, 20 mm — 1450 kg/m³, 40 mm — 1200 kg/m³.
  • Grade → mix ratio uses the standard IS 456 nominal-mix table; M25 is shown indicatively only, per Clause 9.1.
  • Each aggregate size can only be used in one row (duplicates are disabled in the dropdown) until this is confirmed otherwise.
  • Admixture dosage is applied to cement volume, following the source formula, pending confirmation against the "% of cement" label.
